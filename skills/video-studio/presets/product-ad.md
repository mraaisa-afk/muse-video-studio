# Preset — product and service ads

Load when `content_type` is `product_ad`, `goal` is `conversion`, or the user hands you a product photo.

## The only structure that survives 15–30 seconds

```
OBJECTION → DEMO → PROOF → OFFER
```

1. **Objection** (0–3 s): say the thing the buyer is already thinking. "Every bottle sweats."
2. **Demo** (3–12 s): show the mechanism, not the benefits. The ice, the hinge, the drop test.
3. **Proof** (12–22 s): a number, a comparison, or an unfaked before/after.
4. **Offer** (22–30 s): one next action. Price or CTA, not both.

Never open on a logo. Never open on a slow orbit of the product. Open on the problem or the oddest detail.

## Prompt specifics

- **Product lock**: exact silhouette, material, colour, label placement, cap type. Paste it identically into every prompt.
- **One light**: hard key from one side, black or seamless fill. Multiple sources flatten a product.
- **Macro**: 100mm equivalent, f/2.8–f/5.6, focus on the label, background falling off.
- **Motion**: one move — slow orbit, slider, pour, or reveal. Never all four.
- **Hands**: either commit to hands or exclude them (`no hands, no fingers`). Half a hand is the worst outcome.
- **Text**: generate a clean plate, composite the price and legal line in the edit.

## Shot list that works in 20 seconds

| # | Beat | Length | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | objection | 2–3 s | extreme macro on the failure |
| 2 | reveal | 2 s | the product enters frame |
| 3 | demo | 4–6 s | the mechanism, in one unbroken move |
| 4 | proof | 4–5 s | comparison split or a number card |
| 5 | offer | 3–4 s | hero + price/CTA |

## Copy rules

- Title: the objection or the outcome, not the product name alone.
- Description line 1: what it does, in the buyer's words.
- Never claim a result you cannot show.
- Health, finance and earnings claims need a source and a disclaimer; if you cannot source it, cut it.
- Disclose paid partnerships in the first line and with the platform's built-in label.

## Platform notes

| Platform | Length | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | 15–30 s | cover text must survive the 3:4 grid crop |
| TikTok | 15–40 s | native, not polished; UGC style outperforms studio |
| YouTube Shorts | 20–45 s | title carries the search term; description carries the link |
| Facebook | 15–45 s | write for shares; older audience, longer patience |
| LinkedIn | 30–90 s | lead with the business outcome, not the product |

## What the lint will catch

`goal: conversion` without a `cta` beat fails outright. Duration outside the platform window warns. Shot length over the platform cap fails. Thumbnail text that repeats the title warns — a product thumbnail should say the *promise*, not the product name.
